Make it work offline

  1. Connect to Wi‑Fi and open the site in Safari
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Home, Map, Help, Checklist once online
  4. On Help: Download Map for Offline Use
  5. Airplane Mode → open from the Home Screen icon
